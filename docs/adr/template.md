# ADR NNNN: Short decision title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR NNNN
- **Decision date:** YYYY-MM-DD, if known
- **Recorded:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Decision makers:** Owner and implementer, as applicable

## Context

State the problem, constraints and relevant evidence. Separate verified observations from assumptions.

## Decision

State the chosen approach and its scope in terms a future maintainer can use.

## Alternatives

Summarize the material alternatives and why they were not chosen. Avoid inventing a historical
comparison that was not recorded.

## Consequences

State the benefits, operational costs, limitations and any required follow-up.

## Evidence

Link to the relevant plan, code, tests or observed contract. Say explicitly what was run and what
remains unverified. Do not include credentials or private conversation content.
